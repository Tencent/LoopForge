HarnessIcon.jsx adapts the complete official SVG catalog and parent-control interaction
behavior to React. harness-icons.css contains the corresponding animation rules.
SVG geometry and keyframes are preserved from upstream; disabled-state guards and
the React wrapper are local additions. The artwork and animation source also carry
